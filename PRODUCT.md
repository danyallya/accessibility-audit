# Product brief

Problem: Scan a public page, explain findings and let owners rerun the audit.

Audience: small business owners and web teams

## First release

One user role, one core record (audit-runs) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

Automated scan is not certification; limit scope/rate and explain manual review gaps.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
